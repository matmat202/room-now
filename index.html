On midterms. ExploreCourses publishes no exams of any kind — every one of its 20 section types is instructional, and a search for “exam”, “midterm” or “final” across all 3,953 Autumn meetings returns nothing. Stanford’s Bulletin says why: a class holding a midterm outside regular class hours “must announce the date and time during the first week of the academic quarter” — in class, not to a central schedule. So a midterm held during class hours is the class shown here, and one held outside them is not in any public feed. This page will not guess at it.

It also cannot see events, office hours, review sessions, room changes or locked doors. Times are campus time. Looking for an empty room instead? Free classrooms.
